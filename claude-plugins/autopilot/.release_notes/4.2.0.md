## Release Notes

Linear tickets are now automatically moved to "AI Ready" status when a plan is stored, keeping your boards up to date without manual transitions.

## ✨ What's New

### Automatic "AI Ready" Status After Planning

When `/autopilot:linear-plan` stores a plan on a Linear ticket, that ticket now automatically transitions to the "AI Ready" status. Previously, teams had to remember to move tickets manually after planning — now the board reflects execution readiness the moment planning completes.
