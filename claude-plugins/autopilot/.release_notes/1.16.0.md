## [1.16.0](https://github.com/awinogradov/code-assistants/compare/autopilot@v1.15.0...autopilot@v1.16.0) (2026-07-27)

## Release Notes

The `/autopilot:plan` and `/autopilot:run` commands now produce leaner, more actionable plans that stay tightly scoped to what you actually asked for.

## ✨ What's New

### Minimal, Concrete Plans

Plans generated by `/autopilot:plan` and `/autopilot:run` are now constrained to the smallest reliable change that satisfies the request — no extra scope that can't be traced back to the original ask. Implementation steps are written as direct, imperative actions rather than narrative prose, and checkboxes have been removed from step lists. The result is a plan that's easier to follow and less likely to drift into unintended work.
